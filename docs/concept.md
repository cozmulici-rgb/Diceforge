**Game Title Idea: "Facetbound: Echoes of the Void"**  
(Or alternatives: "Diceforge Legacy", "RuneRoller Rogues", "Synergy Dice Descent". I like Facetbound because it evokes swapping faces + binding powerful synergies in a cursed, ever-shifting dungeon.)

### Core Pitch (30-second hook)
You’re a “Facetwalker” — a rogue dice-smith trapped in the ever-rewinding Void Labyrinth. Every run you scavenge dice **Bodies**, **Faces**, and **Runes**, then forge them into living dice that literally roll your actions in combat. Roll a perfect 6 on a flaming skull face with an “Explosion” rune and the entire screen erupts. Miss-roll and your own dice backfire hilariously. It’s Dicey Dungeons meets Slay the Spire meets Binding of Isaac, but the dice are your character, your spells, and your weapons all at once.

### Gameplay Loop (Roguelike Structure)
- **Run Start** → Choose starting “Archetype” (e.g., Pyroclast = fire bodies, Chronomancer = reroll runes, Bonecaster = skull faces).
- **Exploration** (2D top-down pixel art dungeon, rooms connected by doors, fog of war that reveals on successful “Scout” rolls).
- **Combat** (turn-based but dice-driven):
    - You have 3–6 active dice in your “Pool”.
    - Each turn you **roll them all at once**.
    - Assign each die’s result to one of your prepared **Actions** (faces determine what the die *can* do; the number determines power).
- **Events & Shops** → Sacrifice dice parts for better ones, gamble in “Roll-or-Ruin” mini-games, meet cursed NPCs who offer permanent meta-upgrades.
- **Boss** → Massive enemy with its own dice pool that counters yours.
- **Death** → All dice shatter… but you keep **Echo Shards** (meta-currency) to unlock new base Bodies/Runes/Faces for future runs.

Permadeath is brutal because you lose your exact dice build each time — but the joy is in discovering new broken synergies every run.

### The Dice-Building Trinity (The Heart of the Game)
Every die is assembled in a drag-and-drop “Forge” menu between floors.

1. **Bodies** (the chassis — 8–12 types per run, more unlocked meta)
    - Standard D6 (balanced)
    - Heavy D6 (higher average but slower “charge” time)
    - Crystal D8 (extra face slot, fragile — shatters on 1)
    - Bone D4 (low faces but every roll triggers a passive skull effect)
    - Living Flesh D6 (heals itself when you roll low)
    - Void D20 (wildly swingy, starts with a curse rune locked in)

2. **Faces** (6–20 swappable sides, collected as loot)
    - Attack: “Skull Strike” (deals roll × 2 damage)
    - Defense: “Mirror Shield” (blocks roll damage + reflects half)
    - Utility: “Portal Step” (teleport = roll distance)
    - Spell: “Frost Nova” (freezes enemies in radius = roll)
    - Wild: “Chaos Joker” (copies the effect of any other face you own)
    - Legendary: “Dragon Eye” (on 6: instant 50 damage + burn everything)

3. **Runes** (socketed into Body or specific Faces — think enchantments)
    - “Cascade” — if this die rolls 6, reroll ALL your dice for free.
    - “Symbiosis” — this face gains +1 power for every matching symbol on other dice.
    - “Backlash” — on 1, deal damage to yourself but gain a permanent +10% to all future dice of this type (risk/reward).
    - “Echo” — after use, the face stays on the die for the next roll too.
    - “Resonance” — pairs with another specific rune for insane combos (e.g., Fire + Frost = Steam Explosion that hits everything).

**Synergy Examples** (the dopamine hits):
- Body: Crystal D8 + Face: Frost Nova + Rune: Cascade → Roll a 6 → Nova freezes screen → Cascade rerolls everything → chain 4 novas in one turn.
- Body: Bone D4 + Face: Skull Strike (×4 because all faces are skulls) + Rune: Symbiosis → One die deals 4 × (roll × 2) damage. Turns into a machine-gun.
- “Living Dice” build: Flesh body + self-heal faces + Echo rune → you literally cannot die as long as you keep rolling above 2.

### Spell Drafting & Combat Depth
Instead of a deck, you have a **Spell Codex**. Each floor you draft 1–3 new “Action Slots”:
- Slot 1: “Main Attack” (must assign at least one die every turn)
- Slot 2: “Utility” (movement, buff, debuff)
- Slot 3: “Ultimate” (costs all dice showing 6 to activate — massive payoff)

Enemies also roll dice! A Goblin has a pathetic D4 “Stab” die. A Dragon has a D20 “Breath” die with a “Burn on 15+” rune. You can **steal** enemy faces mid-fight if you roll a critical “Disarm” action.

### Progression & Replayability
- **Meta-Progression**: Echo Shards buy permanent unlocks in the “Eternal Forge” hub (new Bodies, rare rune sets, starting archetypes).
- **Curses & Blessings**: Random global modifiers each run (“All dice start cursed — 1s explode for self-damage” vs “Every 6 grants a free face swap”).
- **Achievements** = New starting dice sets (e.g., “Beat the game with only D4s” unlocks the “Tetrahedron God” body).
- **Daily Void** mode: Fixed seed with weekly leaderboards for “most broken synergy score”.

### Visual & Audio Fantasy
- 2D pixel art with gorgeous rolling dice physics (they tumble, bounce, spark, crackle).
- When synergies trigger, screen shakes + particle explosions + the dice literally fuse mid-air for a second.
- Sound design: Every face has its own voice line when it rolls high (“Dragon Eye”: deep roar). Low roll: sad “aww” from the dice itself.

### Why This Will Hook Players
- Infinite build variety (literally billions of combinations).
- “Just one more run” because you’re always 3 rooms away from that perfect rune drop.
- Satisfying risk/reward: Do you reroll that 1 or keep the safe 4?
- Feels fair — bad luck can be mitigated by smart building, but perfect rolls feel godlike.
